Put your own CDM here.
========================

This folder is where the script looks for its Widevine device file.

It must be named exactly:

    LastPerson07.wvd

Full path:

    LastPerson07/LastPerson07.wvd

I am not shipping one and I will not give you one. Dump your own from a
device you own. Without this file the script cannot decrypt anything -
there is no workaround and no public one that still works.

If you see this file in a repo you cloned, that is expected. It is just a
placeholder so the folder exists.
